03 · How it's structured

How it's structured

Three kinds of work, priced three ways.

Marketing proposals get confusing because they blend one-time projects, ongoing services, and ad budget into a single number. We keep them separate. Everything here falls into one of three buckets.

BUILD

Lasting assets & systems

One-time work that creates lasting assets: brand, Google profile, content engine, the launch shoot, the funnels.

Fixed project fee, paid once.
RUN

The work that never stops

Ongoing work: posting, monthly shoots, reviews, newsletter, reporting.

Monthly retainer. You choose a tier.
SPEND

Your ad budget

Advertising budget paid directly to Google & Meta. Managed for a small percentage fee, never marked up.

On your account. Management billed as a %.
“Running Google Ads” and “a video shoot at the gym” were never competing options.

One is Run, one is Build, and the budget itself is Spend. You're not choosing between them, you're choosing how much of each.
