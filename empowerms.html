01 · The problem

Good content, lost in the structure

With the launch of EPIC, the Empower Policy & Innovation Center, research and policy now sit at the heart of your mission. The site was built in 2017 and was never structured to carry that. The root cause is navigation, not content.

01

Everything funnels through "Policy"

An op-ed, a research report and a policy position all look the same and live in the same place. A lawmaker, donor or journalist arriving for evidence cannot tell them apart, so they leave instead of reading.

02

A 2017 information architecture

The navigation was built for a smaller library. As the volume of Op-eds and Research grew, the menu was never re-thought, so it now carries far more than it can clearly hold.

03

The look reads its age

A 2017 visual language undersells the rigour of the research, and it is drifting from the refreshed messaging you are rolling out now.

What is not broken

The content. This engagement does not rewrite or re-platform anything. It stays in WordPress and reorganises what you already have so it is easy to navigate and good to look at.

02 · Scope & deliverables

Phase 1, the refresh

Delivered end to end by one person, design, frontend and WordPress build, inside your one to three month window.

Navigation & IA restructure

A re-mapped sitemap and menu that give Op-eds, Research and Policy distinct, obvious homes.

Performance PRIORITY

Fast load times are a core goal of this project, not an afterthought. Lean theme, optimised assets, measured against Core Web Vitals.

SEO & AEO PRIORITY

Search and answer-engine optimisation so your research gets found in Google and surfaced, and cited, by AI answer engines.

Visual refresh

A refreshed theme, type, colour and spacing, aligned to your new EPIC-era messaging and applied site-wide.

Content templates

Purpose-built layouts for Op-eds, Research and Policy, plus a refreshed homepage and listing pages.

Filtering & search

Filter the Research archive by issue, type and year, with fast on-site search. Included as standard.

Accessibility

A WCAG-minded pass so the site is usable for every Mississippian, on any device.

WordPress theme build

Implemented in your existing WordPress so the team keeps publishing exactly as they do today.

Launch & handover

QA, deploy and a short walkthrough so the team owns the new theme with confidence.

03 · Approach

Structure first, surface second

We fix the map before we paint the rooms. The navigation and IA get settled and signed off early, so every design and build decision after that reinforces a structure we already know works. It mirrors your own advocacy model: define the problem, then build the solution on solid ground.

04 · Timeline · roughly 6 to 9 weeks

WK 1-2

Discovery & IA

Audit the current site and analytics, map a new sitemap, and agree the navigation that separates Op-eds, Research and Policy. You sign off the structure before any design begins.

WK 3-5

Design

Refresh the visual theme to match your new messaging, then design the bespoke templates for each content type alongside the homepage and listing pages.

WK 6-8

WordPress build

Build the approved designs into your existing WordPress as a clean, fast theme. Performance, SEO and AEO, and accessibility are handled here, with QA throughout.

WK 9

Launch

Final checks, deploy to production, and a short walkthrough so your team can publish confidently from day one.

One point of contact throughout. You review at the end of each stage. No decision-by-committee, no surprises mid-build.

05 · Investment

Phase 1, fixed price

$12K to $17k

A single fixed price, agreed up front. Where you land in this range comes down to a few clear factors, not a menu of paid extras.

What moves the price within the range

$12K$17k
01

Number of bespoke page templates

The main driver. The core set, homepage plus Op-eds, Research and Policy, sits near $12K. Each further unique template, for example About, Issues, Events, People or Donate, moves you toward $17k.

02

Whether wireframing is needed

If you want to see and sign off the full structure as clickable wireframes before visual design begins, that adds design time.

03

Scale of content migration

Sorting the existing archive into the new Op-eds, Research and Policy structure by hand. This is what lets us design pages specifically for each type of content. The larger the archive, the more this adds.

Once we confirm these, the price is fixed. 50% to start, 50% on launch. No hourly billing.
