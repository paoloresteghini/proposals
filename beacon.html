SEO & AI discovery

Make the research easier to find.

Organic search brought 46,004 US sessions to Center last year. Protect those entrances and make your reports, cases and expertise easier to discover.

What the initial audit found

The tested Center homepage lacks a meta description. The audit also flags nine links without descriptive text. These are specific fixes, not a complete measure of search visibility.

Fix the technical foundations

Review titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, crawl controls and sitemaps. Build appropriate structured data into the templates and give links meaningful labels.

Connect authoritative content

Publish readable HTML reports with clear authors, dates and sources. Link related research, policy and cases through issue pages so readers and search engines can follow the subject.

Protect search during migration

Preserve useful URLs, map changed addresses and check redirects, internal links and indexability before launch.

Verify the result

Check representative templates and crawl the rebuilt site. With Search Console access, establish query and indexing baselines and check the new sitemap and key pages after launch.

The archive

Sort once. Publish consistently.

Your archive goes back to 2009. The public Center sitemap currently lists 1,678 posts and 57 pages. Reorganizing that library needs a reviewable process.

1. Inventory the content

Capture each URL, title, date, content type and issue. Identify duplicate items, old campaigns, documents and custom layouts before deciding what moves.

2. Propose the new structure

Use AI-assisted analysis to suggest content types and issue tags. Return an Excel review sheet so your team can correct the classification and approve retention decisions.

3. Apply the approved map

Move retained content into its agreed publishing fields and templates. Preserve useful addresses and add redirects wherever an address changes.

4. Check representative content

Review reports, cases, media, older posts and forms after migration. Reconcile the inventory so retained content is accounted for.

Agree the exceptional work up front

The archive review and approved classification are included. Agree the set of legacy layouts, historic PDF conversions and interactive tools before fixing the fee. The HTML report template supports future publishing from day one.

The year 2009 comes from the call; URL counts come from public sitemaps on 6 October 2026. Nothing is retired without your approval.
