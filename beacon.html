Included scope

A site your team can own.

I handle structure, design, build and launch. Approved features are included in the fixed build fee.

Structure & content

Issue hubs, policy and case libraries, newsroom and search by issue/type. Dedicated layouts for reports, cases, releases, blogs and op-eds.

Visual design & editing

A consistent visual system across the homepage and page templates, built in Elementor so your team can edit and publish.

Reports, wins & media

HTML reports with contents, reading progress, sources and PDFs. Flourish where useful, approved impact counters and click-to-load hosted video.

Forms & measurement

Issue-tagged Mailchimp signups, verified forms and donation handoffs. Measure signup page/channel and completed donations where the platform supports confirmation.

SEO & migration

Page titles, descriptions, authors, dates, structured data, duplicate-content checks and redirects. Full crawl, sitemap checks and Search Console baseline in discovery; access required.

Speed & accessibility

Optimize images, fonts, scripts and embeds. Fix contrast, headings, language, zoom and labels. Test keyboards/forms and compare template performance before and after.

Includes home, about, people, contact, donate and participation pages. Required plugins and custom work are identified in discovery and included in the agreed scope. Search rankings and AI citations cannot be guaranteed.
