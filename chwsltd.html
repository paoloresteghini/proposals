03 · What's covered

What's covered

Three kinds of work, one retainer.

Everything the retainer does falls into one of three buckets. Knowing which is which keeps it clear what's included month to month, and what counts as a larger, separately-quoted project.

MAINTAIN

Keep it safe & current

WordPress core, theme & plugin updates, security and backups. Laravel Repository and calculator upkeep, dependency updates, and hosting kept healthy.

Routine, done on a schedule.
SUPPORT

Fix & change as needed

General bug fixing across the site, Repository and calculator, plus submittal PDF changes and small content or configuration tweaks.

Reactive, within reasonable use.
EVOLVE

Add new products

Adding and configuring new products in the catalogue and calculator. Straightforward additions are included; larger new features are quoted separately.

Standard & Priority tiers.

How the work is measured

  • Unlimited reasonable use. No hours to count or carry over. Bring me the everyday changes as they come up and they're covered by your monthly fee.
  • Fair-use, not a loophole. "Reasonable" means the steady flow of maintenance, fixes, PDF edits and product additions — not a full redesign or a brand-new system dressed up as a tweak.
  • Bigger projects are quoted up front. Anything substantial gets its own fixed quote before any work starts, so there are never surprise bills.
